How to Submit Requirements

1

Prepare Your Documents

Make sure all required documents are clear and readable (PDF, JPG, or PNG format).

2

Click Upload Button

Click the "Upload" button next to the requirement you want to submit.

3

Select File

Choose the file from your computer and click "Submit".

4

Wait for Verification

After all requirements are submitted, your enrollment will be reviewed by the registrar within 3-5 business days.
